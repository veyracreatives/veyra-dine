Cancellation and refund policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy applies to purchases of Veyra Dine In plans from Veyra Creative & Digital Lab.

Try before you pay

Every restaurant gets a 10-day free trial with all features, so you can check that Veyra Dine In fits your restaurant before paying.

Refunds

  • Within 7 days of payment: if Veyra Dine In does not work for you, write to us and we will refund the full amount. Your plan is then stopped.
  • After 7 days: payments are not refundable, because the license has been delivered and used.
  • Paid twice or charged the wrong amount: we refund the extra amount in full.

Cancellation

  • Monthly and Yearly plans: you can cancel any time; the plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for and is not renewed.
  • Lifetime license: a one-time purchase with no renewal to cancel. Update renewals are optional.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are sent back to the account you paid from within 5 to 7 working days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

Delivery

Veyra Dine In is software delivered online. Nothing is shipped. When you pay online, your plan starts straight away and the receipt is sent to your email.

How to ask

Email us at marketing@veyracreatives.com with your restaurant name and payment reference (receipt number or Razorpay payment ID).
