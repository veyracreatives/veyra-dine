Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information Veyra Creative & Digital Lab ("we") collects through Veyra Dine In and this website, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Restaurant owners: name, restaurant name, phone number, email, address, menu, prices and photos you add, and your orders and sales records.
  • Guests who order: name, mobile number or email, and a password if the restaurant asks guests to log in; the dishes they order and payments they mark as paid.
  • Sign in with Google: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We use them only to create and sign in to your account. We do not get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.
  • Payments: you pay by UPI or bank transfer, or through an online payment provider when available. We keep the amount, date and payment reference. We never see or store full card numbers, UPI PINs or bank passwords.
  • Technical data: basic server logs (IP address, browser type, time) to keep the service secure and working, and anonymous visit totals (see Website statistics).

How we use it

  • To run the service: show menus, send orders to the restaurant, split bills and print receipts.
  • To manage your account, license and payments, and to send service messages by email.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell personal information. A restaurant can see the details of guests who order at that restaurant. We share data only with service providers that help us run Veyra Dine In (hosting, payment processing), or when required by law.

Storage and security

Data is stored on servers in India. Passwords are stored encrypted (hashed). Connections to the website use HTTPS.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information by emailing us at marketing@veyracreatives.com. Restaurant data is deleted within 30 days after an account is closed, except records we must keep by law.

Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising cookies.

Website statistics

We count visits to this website with our own tool, hosted on our server in India. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel or advertising cookies. Your browser keeps a random visitor number (in local storage, not a cookie), and we record the pages and parts of the page you view, the buttons you click, how long you stay, the website that sent you, your type of device and browser, and your approximate city, worked out from your internet address. The internet address itself is not stored, and we never record what you type into forms.

If you create an account, your earlier visits are linked to that account so we can see how restaurants find us. Visit records are deleted after 400 days and are not shared with anyone. To stop being counted on a device, open dinein.veyracreatives.com/?notrack once. City data: IP Geolocation by DB-IP.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
