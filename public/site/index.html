Questions

Good to know

Do my guests need to download an app?

No. They open the phone camera, scan the QR on the table and the menu opens in their browser. It works on Android and iPhone.

What do I need at the restaurant?

A Windows 10 or 11 computer (or laptop) at the counter with internet. Keep the app open and you'll hear every new order. Your staff can also open the order board on their phones.

Can I run my restaurant from my phone?

Yes. Log in on any Android phone or iPhone and add Dine In Admin to your home screen. It works like an app: new orders ring and pop up, and you can accept orders, print the KOT and bill, and see sales by day and month. No Windows computer is needed, and several phones can be logged in at once.

Do guests need to join my Wi-Fi?

No. Your menu is hosted online, so it opens on mobile data too. If you like, you can still show your WiFi password on the menu.

Can it run without a waiter taking orders?

Yes. Turn on "Accept new orders automatically" and every order from a table goes straight to the kitchen and prints its KOT. Turn on "Guests pay first" and guests pay by UPI from their phone before the food is made. Your staff only serve the food.

Can my waiters take orders on their phones?

Yes. The restaurant admin sets a waiter PIN in Settings › Waiters and gives each waiter the restaurant ID, the PIN and the waiter link. Waiters log in with those and their own name, then see every table, take orders, hear calls from tables and know when food is ready. It works on any Android phone or iPhone, with no app to install.

What happens when the internet goes down?

Your counter keeps working. You can take counter orders, print KOTs and bills, change order status and mark payments while offline, and everything is sent to the server by itself when the internet is back. With the Windows app, WiFi backup goes further: the laptop takes over in about a minute, guests scan the backup QR on the table and waiters keep taking orders on your own WiFi. Keep the laptop on and connected to your WiFi.

How do loyalty points work?

Turn on loyalty points in Settings and choose how much a guest spends for one point, what a point is worth and the minimum to redeem. Guests enter their phone number when they order, and points are added when the table is cleared. At billing, look up the number and use the points as payment. You can switch it off any time.

Can guests see our WiFi password?

Only if you want them to. Add your network name and password in Settings and turn it on. Guests then see a WiFi button on the menu with the password, a copy button and a QR code that joins the network.

How do the games and leaderboard work?

Turn on Games in Settings. Guests tap "Play and win" on the menu while they wait. The best scores each week show on your menu, and the week's winner gets the prize you set, like a free dessert or bonus loyalty points. Games are always free to play. Only first names and scores are shown, never phone numbers.

What does the lifetime plan include?

You pay once and the app is yours to use for as long as you like, with online hosting for your menu. Updates and new features are included for the first year. After that your app keeps working as it is, and you can renew updates whenever you want them.

What happens after the 10-day free trial?

The app asks you to choose a plan. Pay online for monthly, yearly or lifetime and it starts straight away, with no key to enter. Your menu, tables and QR codes stay exactly as they were, so there's nothing to set up again.

Can I change my menu and prices any time?

Yes. Edit dishes, prices and photos, or mark something sold out. Guests see the change straight away and your QR codes stay the same.

Do you work with restaurants near me?

We work with restaurants, cafés and cloud kitchens across Navi Mumbai, Thane and Mumbai, and with restaurants anywhere in India over email and video call.

Can you set it up for me?

Yes. Send us your menu and we'll add the dishes and photos and help you print the QR cards. Email us at marketing@veyracreatives.com.

What is a QR code ordering system?

It is a digital menu that guests open by scanning a QR code on the table. They see dishes with photos and prices, place the order from their own phone, and the order reaches your counter with the table number. No waiter has to write it down, so orders are faster and there are fewer mistakes.

Can it print KOT and bills?

Yes. Veyra Dine In prints kitchen order tickets (KOT) and customer bills on 58 mm and 80 mm thermal printers, with your GSTIN, FSSAI number and a UPI QR code on the bill. You can also print KOTs automatically for every new order.

Can guests pay by UPI and split the bill?

Yes. Guests at the same table can order together, then split the bill equally or by what each person ate, and pay their share by UPI to your own account. You see who has paid at the counter.
